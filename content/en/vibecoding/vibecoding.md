## [EightyFull 八分饱](/tools/eightyfull/)

A meal-pacing timer that nudges you to stop eating near your target "fullness" point — the alert fires at an unpredictable moment drawn from a probability distribution instead of a fixed time, so you can't anticipate and defeat it. Draggable custom distribution shapes, a bilingual EN/中文 UI, and a few too many alert-sound options. Built with Claude Code.
